# CivAware

CivAware is a free, community-first learning platform built to make people safer in both the **physical world** and the **digital world**. It teaches everyday life skills the way people actually learn — by placing an unfamiliar danger side-by-side with a real-world scenario they already understand instinctively.

No sign-up. No database. No cost to the user or the developer — just practical knowledge, available in **English, Hindi, and Punjabi**.

## 🎯 The Aim

CivAware is not the aim itself , it is one step toward a longer goal: a world that is just as safe and interactive in the digital space as it is in the physical one.

Every module exists to close the same gap , between a danger people can *see* and one they can't.

---

## 💻 E-Literacy & Cyber Ethics — Live Now

The first module, and the one currently live, tackles a simple problem: **in the real world, we instantly recognize theft. In the digital world, we don't.**

If a stranger reaches into your pocket, you feel it. If someone breaks your door lock, you see the broken lock. But if your bank account is emptied by someone typing quietly in another city, there's no face, no footsteps, no broken lock — just a machine, and a person hidden completely behind it.

This module makes that invisible thief visible by pairing 53 real cyber-safety topics — phishing, scams, impersonation, viruses, privacy, cyberbullying, misinformation, and digital well-being — with a real-world physical mirror for each one. Every topic is:

- Organized into **8 sub-sections**, searchable and filterable
- Fully **translated** into English, Hindi, and Punjabi
- Readable aloud via built-in **text-to-speech**, for accessibility
- Backed by a **review portal** so learners can give feedback on what worked

## 🛠️ Tech & Setup

CivAware is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework, no build step, no backend.

```
CivAware/
├── index/
│   ├── index.html
│   ├── index.css
│   ├── index.js
│   └── index-data.js
└── Eliteracy/
    ├── eliteracy.html
    ├── eliteracy.css
    ├── eliteracy.js
    └── eliteracy-data.js
```

To run it locally, just open `index/index.html` in a browser — no server, no dependencies, no cost.
